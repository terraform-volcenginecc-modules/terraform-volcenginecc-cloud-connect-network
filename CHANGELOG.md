## [Unreleased]

### Added

- Initial Transit Router based cloud-connect-network module.
- Stable-key VPC, Direct Connect Gateway, VPN, and cross-region peer attachments.
- Custom route tables, route-table associations and propagations, and static attachment and black-hole routes.
- Optional allocation from caller-owned Transit Router bandwidth packages.
- Basic and complete examples for VPC connectivity and multi-region group networking.
